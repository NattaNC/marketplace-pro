## Banner — `/herooffer-minimal`

```text
Create professional artwork for Mercado Libre in 1200x1200 format, using the provided product image.

Product: Mchose V9 Pro Headset
Color: Black

Theme: TRI-MODE: 2.4G + BLUETOOTH + USB-C

Keep the product exactly as it is, without altering its shape, color, proportions, texture, or details.

Visual Style: Minimalist, simple, premium product photography, Nike-style minimalism, ultra-realistic, studio lighting, high contrast, soft shadow, white and light gray background.

Composition: The product should be highlighted, slightly floating, with a soft shadow underneath. The overall look should be modern, technological, clean, and sophisticated.

Do not add exaggerated elements, do not distort the product, do not create new parts, and do not modify the original design.

The artwork must be written in Brazilian Portuguese.

Large title: Montserrat Black
Subtitle: Montserrat Medium
Smaller text: Montserrat Regular or SemiBold

Large and medium text, not small.

Create an image that doesn't look like AI-generated art. I want the subject to be large and centered, the product to cover 10% of the text.

Add icons to the image to make it easier to read.
```

**Adaptação prudente:** a instrução de sobrepor 10% do texto foi interpretada como tolerância máxima, não requisito; manter o texto 100% legível é preferível. A estética de Nike funciona como inspiração genérica de minimalismo e não autoriza copiar identidade de marca.
